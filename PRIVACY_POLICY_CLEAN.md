## 4. Your Rights Under DPDP Act

You have these rights under India's data protection law:

### 4.1 Right to Information
You can ask what data we hold about you.

### 4.2 Right to Correction
You can correct wrong data or complete incomplete data.

### 4.3 Right to Erasure
You can ask us to delete your data. We will delete it within 30 days, or as soon as practicable, unless we are legally required to keep it.

### 4.4 Right to Data Portability
You can request your data in a commonly used electronic format.

### 4.5 Right to Grievance Redressal
You can file a complaint if you have concerns about how we handle your data.

**To exercise these rights, contact our Grievance Officer (see Section 11).**

## 6. How We Protect Your Data

### 6.1 Security Measures

- **Encryption**: All data in transit is encrypted using TLS/SSL
- **Authentication**: Secure JWT tokens with automatic expiration
- **Access Controls**: Only authorized team members can access personal data
- **Regular Reviews**: We review our security practices regularly

### 6.2 Data Retention

We keep your data only as long as needed:
- **Account data**: Until you delete your account
- **Learning progress**: Until you delete your account or request erasure
- **Server logs**: Maximum 1 year

### 6.3 Breach Notification

If a data breach occurs that affects your personal data, we will:
1. Notify the Data Protection Board of India within 72 hours
2. Notify affected users as soon as practicable
