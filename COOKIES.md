# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated:** November 15, 2026

---

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you use websites and applications. They help remember your preferences, enable certain features, and provide analytics.

Alaq uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience.

---

## 2. How We Use Cookies

### 2.1 Essential Cookies (Required)

These cookies are necessary for the Service to function:

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `sb-access-token` | Keeps you signed in | Session |
| `sb-refresh-token` | Refreshes your session | 30 days |
| `supabase-auth-token` | Authentication state | Session |

### 2.2 Functional Cookies

These remember your preferences:

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `alaq-teacher` | Your teacher preference (Zaid/Zara) | 1 year |
| `alaq-language` | Your selected language | 1 year |
| `alaq-theme` | Your theme preference (light/dark) | 1 year |

### 2.3 Analytics Cookies

These help us understand how people use our Service:

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics - distinguishes users | 2 years |
| `_ga_<container-id>` | Google Analytics - session tracking | 2 years |

**Note**: We may use analytics but currently do NOT have Google Analytics or similar tracking enabled. These are included for future use.

### 2.4 Offline Content

We store content locally for offline access:

- Downloaded Qur'an audio files
- Cached hadith translations
- Saved bookmarks and preferences

This uses browser storage (localStorage/IndexedDB), not cookies.

## 3. Managing Cookies

### 3.1 Browser Settings

You can control or disable cookies through your browser:

**Chrome:**
- Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and site data

**Safari:**
- Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data

**Firefox:**
- Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection

**Edge:**
- Settings → Cookies and site permissions

### 3.2 What Happens If You Disable Cookies

Some features may not work properly:

- ❌ You may need to sign in more frequently
- ❌ Your preferences may not be saved
- ❌ Offline features may not work
- ❌ Some pages may not load correctly
