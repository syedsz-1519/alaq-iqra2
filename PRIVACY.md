## 5. Data Storage & Security

### 5.1 Where Your Data Is Stored

- **Database**: Supabase (https://supabase.com), with servers primarily in the United States
- **Authentication**: Supabase Auth for secure user authentication
- **Local Storage**: Your browser or device stores preferences and cached content

### 5.2 Security Measures We Use

- **Encryption**: Data in transit is encrypted using TLS/SSL
- **Authentication**: Secure JWT tokens with automatic expiration
- **Access Controls**: Limited access to personal data by our team
- **Regular Reviews**: We periodically review our security practices

### 5.3 Your Security Responsibilities

- Use a strong, unique password for your account
- Don't share your account credentials
- Sign out when using shared devices
- Keep your device's software updated
